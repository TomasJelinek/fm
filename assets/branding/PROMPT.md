# Image generation brief

Built-in `image_gen` tool, transparent background enabled, 2026-11-06.

## Original prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: desktop application icon for Midnight Explorer, a modern dual-pane file manager. Primary request: a single circular moon, one half light and the other half dark. Centered front-facing full moon disc, a clean slightly curved vertical terminator dividing the two sides into roughly equal areas. Light side cool silver ivory, dark side deep midnight slate blue, visibly dark but still readable. Very restrained broad lunar crater marks, few large subtle shapes, never noisy photographic texture. Elegant polished minimal dimensional illustration with crisp silhouette and smooth shading. True transparent background outside the moon. Square composition, moon occupies 88 percent of canvas, generous equal outer margin. Must read clearly at 16, 24, 32 and 48 pixels and on both dark and light desktops. No words, letters, stars, landscape, border tile, rings, logos, extra objects, or cast shadow outside the disc. One final icon only, not a grid or mockup.

## Final refinement prompt

Edit this desktop app icon. Keep the same circular moon, silver light half, midnight blue dark half, the same curved boundary, surface shading and composition. Fix only the outer silhouette: remove every stray white speck, bright fragment and colored fringe outside the round moon. Make the circumference perfectly smooth, clean and anti-aliased, especially on the left, top, and bottom. Outside the moon must be absolutely transparent with no flecks or halo. Keep a square canvas and balanced margin. Do not add objects or text.
