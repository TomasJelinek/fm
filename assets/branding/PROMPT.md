# Image generation brief

Built-in `image_gen` tool, transparent background enabled. Harvest Navigator is the
default application icon (`master.png` in this folder); Origami Trail and Interleave
are the alternates in `variants/`.

## Selected launcher candidates

The following briefs cover the retained designs and their final refinements.
The built-in image generator used a transparent background for each asset.

### Origami Trail

Use case: precise-object-edit. Keep this transparent purple-and-gold Origami Trail folder icon as drawn. Make a surgical change to its lavender emblem only. Remove both existing upright blocks completely. In that patch place a simple flat pictogram of ONE open desktop file-manager window seen straight on, a single WIDE landscape silhouette about 1.8 times as wide as tall and roughly 46% of the violet front's width. Draw it like an elegant app toolbar icon in lavender ink: one continuous outer outline, one broad uninterrupted title bar connecting both halves across the top, one shared baseline, and ONE lavender vertical divider which starts beneath the title bar. Inside both panes the background is the original dark violet. In the left pane, two simple bold horizontal lavender filename strokes, the lower one shorter; in the right, one short lavender filename stroke and a longer one, giving the two lists different rhythms. Keep stroke widths substantial and simplify to read when the whole icon is shown at 32 px. It must read as ONE split-view software window with two open lists in a common frame. Neither pane has an independent outline; no opaque lavender towers, symmetrical drive slots, second frame, PC stand, feet, labels, words, red/green lights, extra icons, or arrow. Keep its left-of-center placement with breathing room above the gold diagonal. Preserve the original rich violet folder, lilac raised tab, angular golden paper crescent along bottom and right, silhouette, proportions, framing and clear square margins. Genuine alpha outside the icon.

### Harvest Navigator

Keep the deep cobalt cover, curved broad honey-gold lunar fold with its two soft crater dimples, and the bright blue tab exactly as in the reference. In the open cobalt area slightly above and left of center, place one fine, purposeful physical COMPASS NEEDLE pointing northeast, with an icy-silver blue-lit triangular north tip, shorter medium azure south tip, and a small pivot at their meeting point. The needle is a two-ended elongated lozenge with a clear faceted ridge, approximately a third of the folder's width along its diagonal. Set it on an understated, partial blue engraved compass arc if needed for instant recognition. No arrow shaft, airplane shape, cardinal letters or tick marks. Keep the gold fold fully visible and the luminous blue face open around this device. One integrated image.

The final color adjustment used this brief.

Precise color-only edit of the supplied Harvest Navigator launcher icon. Treat the reference as a locked drawing: same centered folder, exact gold crescent profile and crater details, same position and size of the silver-blue compass needle and its partial arc, preserve bevels, alpha, margins, original framing, and all original shading. Adjust ONLY the blue pigment of the folder face and its back tab slightly toward the named color Dodger Blue (target hue reference #1E90FF). Shift the original distinctly violet-leaning ultramarine a little toward a fresher azure and lift the mids modestly. Aim around #1038AD for the main midtone, deeper blue in shadows, naturally brighter on the lips. Keep the cover predominantly rich and deep; let the top bevels and lit edges carry more of the Dodger Blue hue. Avoid a flat sky-blue recolor. This must read as the very same illustration with a gentle blue hue adjustment, including the unaltered warm gold lower crescent. Transparent square RGBA background, no new object, mark, text or surrounding shadow.

### Interleave

Use case: logo-brand. A single original abstract launcher icon for Midnight Explorer, a desktop file manager. Call the design Interleave. The mark itself is the whole icon: two broad interlocking folds of coated paper, one luminous peacock turquoise and one deep midnight teal, with a tiny warm golden underside revealed at their central overlap. Construct a beautiful, deliberate woven join: left turquoise sheet turns back at its upper edge and slips behind the right dark sheet; at the lower edge the dark sheet folds behind the left. An elongated lens-shaped sliver of marigold appears at the crossing. It should be possible to trace the two sheets with the eye. Their facing edges nod to two file panes without any literal window, file, folder, or UI drawing. Freestanding, asymmetrically stepped almost-square outer silhouette, slightly taller on the left, crisp bends plus one gentle scooped inner edge. Wide, simple areas of color and only three or four planes; gold should be at most one eighth of the surface. Graphic and distinctive like a modernist folded-paper sculpture viewed straight on, with restrained satin highlights at the folds and rich color depth. Choose optical clarity and negative space over realism or detail. It has to be identifiable at 16 and 32 px. Center in a square with 10 percent clear margin on each side and TRUE transparent alpha outside. NO backing tile, road, winding stripe, route, location pin, compass, arrows, mountains, badge, letter, logo text, generic infinity symbol, ribbon bow or external shadow. Render just one finished icon.
