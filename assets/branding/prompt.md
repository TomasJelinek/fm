# Image generation brief

Built-in `image_gen` tool, transparent background enabled. Harvest Navigator is the
default application icon (`master.png` in this folder); Origami Trail and Interleave
are the alternates in `variants/`.

The following briefs cover the retained designs and their final refinements.
The built-in image generator used a transparent background for each asset.

### Interleave

The family refinement lifts the shadowed sheet toward deep marine teal. Amber and mint stripes sit on the smooth turquoise fold. A tiny crescent is engraved in the central gold underside, next to a soft violet reflection.

Use case: logo-brand. A single original abstract launcher icon for Midnight Explorer, a desktop file manager. Call the design Interleave. The mark itself is the whole icon: two broad interlocking folds of coated paper, one luminous peacock turquoise and one deep midnight teal, with a tiny warm golden underside revealed at their central overlap. Construct a beautiful, deliberate woven join: left turquoise sheet turns back at its upper edge and slips behind the right dark sheet; at the lower edge the dark sheet folds behind the left. An elongated lens-shaped sliver of marigold appears at the crossing. It should be possible to trace the two sheets with the eye. Their facing edges nod to two file panes without any literal window, file, folder, or UI drawing. Freestanding, asymmetrically stepped almost-square outer silhouette, slightly taller on the left, crisp bends plus one gentle scooped inner edge. Wide, simple areas of color and only three or four planes; gold should be at most one eighth of the surface. Graphic and distinctive like a modernist folded-paper sculpture viewed straight on, with restrained satin highlights at the folds and rich color depth. Choose optical clarity and negative space over realism or detail. It has to be identifiable at 16 and 32 px. Center in a square with 10 percent clear margin on each side and TRUE transparent alpha outside. NO backing tile, road, winding stripe, route, location pin, compass, arrows, mountains, badge, letter, logo text, generic infinity symbol, ribbon bow or external shadow. Render just one finished icon.
